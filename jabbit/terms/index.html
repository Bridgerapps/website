3. What Jabbit does

3.1 Features

Jabbit helps you keep your own records. It lets you:

  • log injections (product, amount, site, time, notes) and see your history;
  • enter your own schedules and get reminders at the times you set;
  • record your vials and see their age and what is left, based on what you log;
  • see which injection sites you used and when;
  • read reference information about compounds from public sources;
  • record weight, measurements and progress photos, with optional read-only import from Apple Health.

Some features, described in section 3.2, are offered only in some countries.

3.2 Features that depend on your App Store country

Which features you see depends on the country or region of your App Store account (your "storefront"), not on where you are. If the storefront is not known yet, Jabbit uses your iPhone's region setting.

In UK, EU, EEA (including Norway, Iceland and Liechtenstein) and Swiss storefronts, Jabbit does not offer:

  • converting a dose into syringe units or a volume to draw;
  • suggested diluent (mixing) volumes;
  • modelled serum-level estimates and charts;
  • suggested doses, dosing plans and dosing content taken from product labels in the library.

Where these features are offered, the conversions are convenience arithmetic based on what you enter, and serum-level charts are theoretical estimates (section 4). We may change which features are offered in a country to comply with local law. If you change your App Store country, the features change with it.

3.3 Your records stay on your iPhone

  • From version 1.0.4, Jabbit stores your records only on your iPhone. It does not sync them between devices or to iCloud, and we never receive them.
  • Your records are included in your iPhone's backups. To move to a new iPhone, use Apple's Quick Start or a backup.
  • You can save a copy of your records as a file with Settings > Export My Data. Photos are not included; they stay in your Photos library.
  • We cannot recover lost records. If you delete the app, or lose or reset your iPhone, without a backup, your records are gone.
  • Data that earlier versions synced to your private iCloud stays there until you delete it. Jabbit no longer reads it. The Privacy Policy explains how to delete it.
