Everything in one private log

Doses, sites, schedules, vials and progress, kept on your iPhone.

Log Each Injection

Record the product, amount, injection site, time and notes. See past and upcoming injections on the calendar.

Site History

Site history across 14 zones, so you can see which areas you used least recently.

Schedules and Reminders

Enter your own schedule: daily, several times a day, chosen weekdays, every few days, or as needed. Reminders at the times you choose.

Dose Phases and Weeks Off

Add dose phases with their own start dates, and weeks on and weeks off, as your prescriber sets them.

Vials

Record what is in each vial, when you opened or mixed it, and when it expires. See each vial's age and how much is left, based on what you log.

Reference Library

Reference entries on 85 compounds with regulatory status, safety warnings and sources, and links to official product information for licensed medicines.

Progress

Weight and body measurements, with optional read-only import from Apple Health. Progress photos saved to your Photos library, with side-by-side comparisons and timelapses.

Export Your Records

Save your records as a file from Settings at any time, to keep or share as you choose.

Stored on Your iPhone

Your records are stored on your iPhone, not on our servers. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Your privacy is not negotiable

Health data is deeply personal. That's why Jabbit was built from the ground up with a privacy-first architecture. Your injection logs, vial data, and progress never touch our servers.

From version 1.0.4, everything stays on your iPhone: Jabbit no longer syncs through iCloud. Export your records to a file from Settings at any time, and move to a new iPhone with Quick Start or a backup.
