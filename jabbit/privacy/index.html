Jabbit Privacy Policy

Effective: October 27, 2026
Applies to: Jabbit for iPhone, version 1.0.4 and later, and the Jabbit pages of bridgerapps.com.

The short version

  • Your records stay on your iPhone. Jabbit stores what you enter on your iPhone only. It does not sync to iCloud and does not send your records to us or to anyone else. Bridger Apps never receives them, and we cannot see, copy or restore them.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. Jabbit has no sign-up, no advertising, and no analytics or advertising tools from other companies. We do not sell personal data.
  • Apple Health is read-only. If you connect it, Jabbit reads only the data types you allow, and never writes to Apple Health.
  • Progress photos are saved to your Photos library. Jabbit keeps small thumbnails on your iPhone.
  • Apple handles payments. We never see your payment details.
  • What we do receive: emails you send us; crash reports and usage statistics Apple passes on, if you have chosen to share them; Apple's sales reports, which do not identify you; and basic logs from our website.

2. What Jabbit keeps on your iPhone

2.1 What it stores

Jabbit stores what you enter or choose to import:

  • Injection records: the product, amount and unit, date and time, injection site, notes, and whether a scheduled dose was taken or skipped.
  • Schedules: the schedules, dose phases, cycles and reminder times you set.
  • Vials: what is in each vial, amounts and volumes you enter, dates, expiry, brand and notes.
  • Profile: display name and photo (both optional), sex, date of birth, height, current and target weight, and your unit and display preferences.
  • Progress: weight, body measurements, body fat percentage, check-ins and notes, including any values imported from Apple Health (section 3).
  • Progress photo records: small thumbnails, the Photos identifiers of the full photos, the date, your weight and the products you were tracking at the time, and any covering or alignment you add (section 4).
  • App settings, and the country of your App Store account (section 6).

Much of this is health information. In UK and EU law, information about your health is "special category" data, which gets extra protection.

2.2 Where it is stored, and backups

  • Jabbit stores your records in its own private storage on your iPhone. If your iPhone has a passcode, iOS encrypts this storage with a key protected by that passcode.
  • Jabbit does not sync between devices. If you used an earlier version on more than one device, each device now keeps its own records.
  • Jabbit's records are part of your iPhone's own backup. If you use iCloud Backup, Apple stores that backup in your iCloud account under Apple's terms; it is end-to-end encrypted only if you have turned on Apple's Advanced Data Protection. If you back up to a computer, you can choose to encrypt the backup with a password.
  • To move to a new iPhone, use Apple's Quick Start or restore from a backup.
  • You can also keep your own copy as a file with Settings > Export My Data (section 10.4).
  • We cannot recover your records. If you delete Jabbit, or lose or reset your iPhone, without a backup, your records are gone for good.

2.3 Data that earlier versions put in your iCloud

Versions of Jabbit before 1.0.4 synced your records through your private iCloud database (Apple's CloudKit). Only your Apple Account could open that database. We never had access to it.

  • Version 1.0.4 and later neither read nor write that data.
  • It stays in your iCloud account until you delete it. To delete it, go to Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage > Jabbit > Delete Data from iCloud. On some iOS versions the menu is called "Manage Account Storage".
  • If an older version of Jabbit is still installed on another device, that device may keep syncing to iCloud until you update or delete it.

2.4 Deleting your records

  • Settings > Delete All My Data in Jabbit deletes all Jabbit records from your iPhone.
  • Deleting the Jabbit app also deletes them from your iPhone.
  • Neither of these deletes:
    • copies in backups you made earlier;
    • export files you saved or shared (section 10.4);
    • photos in your Photos library (section 4);
    • data earlier versions put in iCloud (section 2.3).

3. Apple Health

  • Jabbit connects to Apple Health only if you choose to, using Apple's permission screen. You choose which data types to allow.
  • It can read: biological sex, date of birth, height, weight, body fat percentage and body mass index (BMI). It uses them to fill in your profile and show your progress.
  • Jabbit never writes to or changes Apple Health.
  • Imported values are stored in Jabbit on your iPhone like your other records (section 2). They are not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • We do not use Apple Health data for advertising or marketing, and we do not sell it or use it for data mining.
  • You can stop access at any time in iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Health > Jabbit. When you disconnect inside Jabbit, you can also choose to delete the entries that were imported from Apple Health.

4. Camera and photos

  • Camera: used only when you take a progress photo.
  • Photos access: used to import existing photos and to show photos Jabbit has saved. You can give Jabbit access to selected photos only.
  • Where photos go: full-size progress photos are saved to an album called "Jabbit Progress" in your Photos library. From then on they are handled by the Photos app and, if you have it turned on, by Apple's iCloud Photos. Jabbit keeps small thumbnails and links to those photos in its own storage on your iPhone.
  • Deleting: deleting your data in Jabbit does not delete photos from your Photos library. Delete the album in Photos if you want them gone.
  • Sharing: if you export a comparison image or timelapse, it goes wherever you send it, and the privacy terms of that app or person apply. Jabbit has a tool to cover or blur parts of a photo first. We do not receive anything you share.

5. Reminders

Jabbit schedules reminders as notifications on your iPhone. They are not sent through our servers. A reminder can show the product name and dose on your lock screen. You can change how notifications appear in Settings > Notifications > Jabbit.

6. Your App Store country or region

Some Jabbit features are switched off in some countries because of local rules: in the UK, the EU, the EEA and Switzerland.

  • To decide which features to show, Jabbit asks Apple for the country of your App Store account and remembers it on your iPhone. If that is not available, it uses your iPhone's region setting.
  • This information stays on your iPhone and is not sent to us.
  • Jabbit does not use your location or your IP address.

7. Subscriptions and payments

  • Jabbit Pro subscriptions are sold and charged by Apple. We never see your name, payment details or Apple Account email address.
  • Jabbit checks your subscription status on your iPhone using Apple's StoreKit. We do not run a server that receives your purchase records.
  • Apple gives us sales and subscription reports: for example, the number of trials, subscriptions, renewals and cancellations, by country and date, and revenue. They do not identify you.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple's privacy policy applies to everything Apple does: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

8. Personal data we receive

8.1 Emails you send us

When you email hello@bridgerapps.com, privacy@bridgerapps.com or legal@bridgerapps.com:

  • What we receive: your email address, your name if you include it, what you write and any attachments.
  • Health details: your message may include health information, such as what you take or a problem you had. Please include only what we need to help you. If you do send health information, we use it only to answer you and to keep a record of the conversation.
  • Where it is kept: our email is hosted by Google Workspace (Google LLC), which may store it in the United States and other countries where Google has data centres, and acts only on our instructions.

8.2 Crash reports and usage statistics from Apple

If you have turned on Share with App Developers (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple sends us:

  • crash reports: device model, iOS version, Jabbit version and technical details of the crash;
  • usage statistics: for example, the number of installs and sessions.

Apple says this information does not identify you. It is not designed to contain your Jabbit records. We use it only to fix problems and to understand how the app is used. You can turn sharing off in the same setting.

8.3 Beta testing

If you join a Jabbit beta through Apple's TestFlight, Apple gives us:

  • your name and email address;
  • the feedback and screenshots you send;
  • crash reports from the beta.

We use these only to run the beta.

8.4 App Store ratings and reviews

Reviews you post on the App Store are public and run by Apple. We can read them and reply.

8.5 Our website

  • bridgerapps.com is hosted by Vercel. When you visit, Vercel's servers record technical details such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. These logs are used to deliver and protect the site.
  • The Jabbit pages currently load a font from Google Fonts and a styling script from the Tailwind CSS content delivery network. Those services receive your IP address when the page loads.
  • The site uses no cookies, analytics or advertising.

9. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data, or "share" it for advertising based on what you do on other apps and sites.
  • We do not show ads, track you across other companies' apps or websites, or use analytics or advertising tools from other companies.
  • We do not make automated decisions about you, or profile you.

We disclose the personal data in section 8 only:

  • to the service providers that host our email and website, who act on our instructions;
  • to professional advisers, under a duty of confidentiality;
  • when the law requires it;
  • to a buyer if we sell the business, who must then protect it as this policy says.

On our understanding of the law, Bridger Apps is therefore neither the "controller" nor a "processor" of those records. We still designed Jabbit to protect them, and this policy explains how it works.
  • We are the controller of the personal data described in section 8.
  • Apple is a separate controller for the App Store, payments, iCloud Backup, iCloud Photos, Apple Health and the collection of crash reports and usage statistics.

For the personal data we hold (section 8), you can ask us to:

  • correct it;

  • restrict how we use it;
  • give it to you in a portable format.

  • complain to a data protection authority (section 10.5).

To use these rights, email privacy@bridgerapps.com. We reply within one month. For complex requests the law lets us take up to two more months, and we will tell you if we need them. There is no charge. We will ask for proof of identity only if we need it.

10.4 Your records on your iPhone

We cannot see your Jabbit records, so we cannot give you a copy, correct them or delete them. You do this directly:

  • View and correct: edit any record in the app.
  • Delete: use Settings > Delete All My Data, or delete the app (see section 2.4).
  • Old iCloud data: delete it as described in section 2.3.
  • Export (portability): Settings > Export My Data saves your records as a file (JSON format) and opens the iOS share sheet, so you can keep it or send it where you choose. The file includes your profile (name, sex, date of birth, height and target weight), your tracked products with their schedules and past scheduled doses, your injections, vials and progress entries, and the date, weight and notes of each photo session. It does not include the photos themselves, which stay in your Photos library (section 4), or your app settings. The file contains health information, so Jabbit reminds you of this before the share sheet opens; anyone you send it to can read it. The file is created on your iPhone, and Jabbit deletes its temporary copy when the share sheet closes. We do not receive it unless you send it to us.
